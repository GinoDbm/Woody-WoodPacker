void xtea_encrypt_block(uint32_t v[2], const uint32_t k[4]) :

v[] is a block of 64 bits divided in 2 parts of 32 bits.
k[] is the encryption key of 128 bits divided in 4 parts of 32 bits.
XTEA_ROUNDS is the number of loop iteration
XTEA_DELTA is a constant used for XTEA, it's derived from the golden ratio multiplied by 2^32.

This function performs the XTEA algorithm. Each half of the v[] block gonna be modified but always based on the other part.
We need to get a consequent number of loop iteration to have something strong.

void xtea_decrypt_block(uint32_t v[2], const uint32_t k[4]) :

This function performs the exact opposite of the previous one.
It's here for decrypting our encrypted block.
